# Test your mini app

Run your mini app inside a real Sewa shell. Give it two URLs and press **Start testing**.

| URL | What it is for |
|---|---|
| **Frontend URL** | Where your app is served from. |
| **Backend URL** | Where your app's API calls are sent. |

Your frontend must load the SDK — see [Getting Started](/docs/getting-started).
